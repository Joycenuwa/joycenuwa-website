Love Is Beautiful

Love Is Beautiful explores love beyond the romantic. Across figurative, landscape and abstract painting, the collection considers love as devotion, belonging, nurture, wonder, endurance and connection. Human relationships sit alongside our attachment to heritage, nature, place and the changing seasons.

Through vivid colour, repeated forms, reflected light and contrasting landscapes, the works present love not as a single experience, but a way of recognising value in others, in ourselves, in where we come from, and in the world we inhabit.

Sanctuary in the Waves

ACRYLIC PAINTING

Two figures find one another beneath moonlight while water and sky continue around them. Their intimacy occupies only one moment within a much larger natural world, bringing human connection, moonlight, water and passing time into the same emotional space.

Mother’s Dawn

ACRYLIC PAINTING

Mother’s Dawn explores nurturing love through the intimate relationship between mother and child. Beneath the sheltering branches of a tree, the figures form a quiet image of protection, trust and care. The warm pink and coral sky suggests dawn and the beginning of life, while the surrounding green evokes growth and renewal. The work reflects on a form of love expressed through presence, responsibility and the instinct to protect another as they begin their own journey.

Architecture of the Heart

ACRYLIC PAINTING

Architecture of the Heart considers love as something constructed through experience, connection and memory. Fragmented geometric forms gather around the central heart, suggesting the many encounters and emotions that shape how love is understood and expressed. Contrasting reds, earthy tones and open white spaces evoke both intensity and vulnerability. Rather than presenting the heart as a simple symbol of affection, the work imagines love as an evolving structure, complex, imperfect and continually reshaped.

Unapologetic Devotion

ACRYLIC PAINTING

A solitary figure occupies the composition with quiet authority, surrounded by bold passages of red, black, green and gold. Her raised gesture and closed eyes turn attention inward rather than toward an external audience. Unapologetic Devotion considers self-love as an act of recognition, the freedom to inhabit one's identity, body, culture and presence without seeking permission or validation.

What She Carried Forward

WATERCOLOUR AND ACRYLIC PAINTING

What She Carried Forward explores love through cultural inheritance and the connections between generations of women. Two figures occupy the same visual space, suggesting how identity, knowledge, memory and strength are passed forward and transformed by those who inherit them. The recurring reds, greens, yellows and earthy tones draw from colours widely present across African flags, extending the relationship between the women beyond family to a broader sense of African heritage and collective identity. Their overlapping forms suggest that what one generation carries does not disappear, but continues through those who come after.

To Heritage, With Love

ACRYLIC PAINTING

A contemplative figure appears alongside the African continent, connecting personal presence with cultural memory. Pattern, adornment and silhouette consider heritage as a living relationship: something inherited, carried, questioned and continually reinterpreted. It is a love letter not only to origin, but to the histories that continue to shape identity.

Where the Land Remembers

ACRYLIC PAINTING

Where the Land Remembers considers the connection between people, place and cultural memory. A solitary figure looks across a warm landscape of distant mountains, scattered trees and a glowing sun, suggesting a relationship with land that extends beyond physical presence. The open terrain becomes a space for memory, heritage and belonging, inviting reflection on the places that shape identity and continue to hold meaning across distance and time.

Higher Together

ACRYLIC PAINTING

Higher Together explores love as solidarity, the quiet act of helping one another move forward. Four figures ascend a steep landscape hand in hand, each relying on the person ahead while supporting the one behind. The vast sun and warm African-inspired landscape transform the climb into a metaphor for life's shared journeys, where progress is not always achieved alone. Through connection, encouragement and collective strength, the work presents love not simply as romance, but as the willingness to rise together.

Rooted Through Change

ACRYLIC PAINTING

Rooted Through Change reflects on resilience through the image of a solitary palm bending across a changing sky. Although shaped by its surroundings, the tree remains firmly connected to the earth beneath it. The work considers love for the natural world alongside a broader idea of endurance, the ability to adapt to change without losing one's foundations.

Through the Seasons

ACRYLIC PAINTING

Through the Seasons explores the enduring relationship between time, colour and the natural world. Mountains remain grounded beneath a shifting sky, while their reflections dissolve into the water below. Changes in red, green, yellow and orange suggest different seasons and emotional states existing within a continuous landscape. The work invites reflection on the beauty of transformation and on the forms of attachment that endure even as the world around them changes.

Duality of Affection

ACRYLIC PAINTING

The work celebrates the beauty of contrast in nature. The Contrasting halves of the landscape bring together night and warmth, stillness and intensity, darkness and illumination existing within the same world.

The Complexity of Passion

ACRYLIC PAINTING

Repetition, interruption and changing colour translate emotion into an abstract visual language. The work embraces passion as something that refuses perfect order, layered with intensity, contradiction, movement and moments that cannot always be easily named.
