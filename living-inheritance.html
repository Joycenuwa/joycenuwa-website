Living Inheritance

Living Inheritance explores the ways identity, culture and lived experience continue through people, objects, gestures and everyday traditions. Through photography and digital intervention, the series reflects on what is carried forward, reshaped and made visible across time. The works consider inheritance not as something fixed, but as an evolving relationship between the past, the present and the lives we continue to build.

Keeper of Tides

Digital photography

Inspired by West African textile traditions, this work portrays a guardian standing between memory and water. Water becomes a metaphor for continuity, carrying heritage across generations while allowing identity to evolve.

Ancestral Threads

Digital photography

Cracks become spaces for transformation rather than signs of damage. The work reflects resilience and the possibility of rebuilding through periods of uncertainty.

Contemplative Rhythm

Digital photography

Inspired by West African musical traditions, this work explores rhythm as a keeper of cultural memory. The bata drum becomes both instrument and vessel, carrying stories across generations.

Reflective Presence

Digital photography

Reflective Presence explores the unfinished nature of self-understanding. Fragmented spaces leave room for uncertainty, possibility and the freedom to remain continually in progress.

Vibrant Tones

Digital photography

Colour and music become languages of inheritance. The talking drum connects past and present, suggesting that culture survives through continual performance, adaptation and renewal.

Sacred Adornment

Digital Photography

Sacred Adornment explores the intersection of cultural identity and personal expression. Through layered imagery and symbolic motifs, the work celebrates the beauty and significance of traditional adornment in contemporary contexts.

Eewo (Forbidden)

Digital mixed media

Drawing on the Yorùbá concept of prohibition, ÈÈWÒ explores how inherited systems of control can obscure identity, using language itself as a barrier between visibility and silence.

Unspoken Judgement

Digital Photography

Unspoken Judgement examines the weight of societal expectations and the internal conflicts they create. Through subtle visual cues and symbolic elements, the work explores the effects of judgement on identity and self-worth.

Inherited Silence

Digital Photography

This work reflects on the expectations placed upon women to remain silent. Through layered imagery, it considers how obedience and restraint are learned, inherited and internalised across generations.

Symphony of the Unspoken

Digital Photography

Multiple voices surround a single mouth, exploring silence not as absence but as a space filled with memory, emotion and unspoken truths. The work asks how identity is shaped by both what we express and what we choose to leave unsaid.
