Daily Bread

Daily Bread explores the dignity of labour through the lives of ordinary people whose work quietly sustains families, communities and society. From farming and fishing to trading, transport, craft and food vending, the series honours occupations that often go unnoticed yet form the foundation of everyday life. Through painterly digital manipulation, familiar scenes become visual tributes to resilience, perseverance and purpose.

Threads of Care

Digital Photography

Within the intimacy of the salon, skilled hands transform strands of hair into expressions of identity and culture. The work celebrates patience, craftsmanship and the labour that nurtures confidence while providing a livelihood.

Harvest of Hope

Digital Photography

A lone farmer tends the earth with quiet determination, trusting that today’s labour will become tomorrow’s harvest. The work honours agriculture as a foundation of survival, where perseverance and hope are cultivated together.

Hands of Exchange

Digital Photography

Fresh produce changes hands in a familiar marketplace, capturing the everyday exchanges that sustain seller and buyer. The work celebrates informal commerce as a network of resilience, community and economic survival.

City in Motion

Digital Photography

Crowded roads and yellow buses reveal the relentless rhythm of urban life. The work reflects the movement of people, opportunity and commerce, recognising transportation as one of the invisible engines of the modern city.

The Weight of Provision

Digital Photography

A market trader reaches confidently towards her produce, embodying generations of women whose labour feeds families and strengthens local economies. The work honours the resilience and determination carried through everyday trade.

Embers of Labour

Digital Photography

As evening descends, glowing charcoal illuminates the dedication of a roadside food vendor whose work continues after sunset. The piece reflects endurance and the sacrifices made by those whose livelihoods thrive in the night.

Casting Tomorrow

Digital Photography

A fisherman casts his net across open water, balancing experience with uncertainty. The work symbolises patience, resilience and faith, reminding us that survival often depends upon both effort and chance.
