After the Sun Sleeps

After the Sun Sleeps explores life, labour and human connection after dark. Through the interplay of darkness, artificial light and human presence, the series reveals the quiet communities, routines and overlooked beauty that emerge when daylight fades, inviting viewers to reconsider the night as a space where stories continue to unfold.

Between Worlds

Photography

Seen through glass, the illuminated skyline merges with interior reflections, blurring the boundary between public and private space. The work explores how night reshapes perception, revealing multiple worlds within a single moment.

When the Streets Awaken

Photography

Viewed from above, an evening market transforms an ordinary street into a vibrant gathering place. The work celebrates the temporary communities, movement and connection that emerge after sunset.

Silent Observer

Photography

An empty bench overlooks a city illuminated by thousands of lights. Suspended between solitude and connection, the work invites reflection on the quiet beauty and unseen lives that continue after dark.

Golden Reflection

Photography

Photographed from the Danube, glowing architecture and shimmering reflections transform Budapest into a place of stillness, revealing the quiet beauty that emerges when daylight fades.

Crossing Into Evening

Photography

Tram lines, streetlights and moving figures converge within a shared moment of transition. The work reflects on movement, routine and the subtle transformations that unfold as day gives way to night.

Last Light

Photography

A fading sun rests behind the mountains, casting a path of gold across the water. Suspended between day and night, the work reflects on stillness, change and the quiet beauty of endings.
